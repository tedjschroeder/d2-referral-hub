## SEO Best Practices Applied

1. **Meta Tags:** All landing pages have optimized titles, descriptions, keywords
2. **Headline Structure:** H1-H6 hierarchy for readability and ranking
3. **Content Length:** 1,500+ words per blog post (target)
4. **Internal Linking:** Natural links from blog posts to landing pages
5. **CTA Placement:** Multiple CTAs above and below fold
6. **Mobile Optimization:** Responsive design across all pages
7. **Site Structure:** Logical navigation, breadcrumbs on index
8. **UTM Parameters:** All referral links tagged for attribution
